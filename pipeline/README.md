## Incident Types

* **Extreme Heat**: Severe meteorological or ambient outdoor heat exposure resulting in physiological heat-related illness, such as heat exhaustion, heat stroke, dehydration, or hyperthermia.
* **Lightning**: Direct or immediate atmospheric lightning strikes occurring during storms or outdoor work.
* **Flood**: High-water environmental inundation, such as flash floods, storm surges, or overflowing bodies of water.
* **Wildlife**: Physical encounters with living animals, insects, reptiles, or arachnids resulting in bites, stings, attacks, or trampling.
* **Soil Saturation**: Geotechnical ground failures and earth collapses triggered by saturated or unstable earth, including trench cave-ins, mudslides, and sinkholes.
* **Wildfire**: Burns, smoke inhalation, or entrapment caused by uncontrolled brush, forest, or grass fires and prescribed agricultural burns.
* **Other (None of the above)**: General occupational injuries originating from non-environmental hazards, such as machinery, routine slips and falls, vehicle accidents, and industrial equipment.
